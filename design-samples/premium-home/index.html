Process

A clear path from idea to launch.

Each phase has a purpose, a decision to make, and a visible deliverable before the project moves forward.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the business, offer, audience, current website, and the action the project needs to support.

    Plan

    Organize the sitemap, page structure, content priorities, and conversion flow before design begins.

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Launch

    Test mobile layouts, speed, SEO details, forms, automations, links, and final content before handoff.
