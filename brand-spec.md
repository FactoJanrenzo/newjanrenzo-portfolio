# Janrenzo Facto Brand Spec

## Identity

- Browser favicon: `public/favicon.svg`
- Header and footer avatar: `public/brand/janrenzo-avatar.webp`
- Brand avatar: `public/brand/janrenzo-avatar.webp`
- Primary portrait: `public/brand/janrenzo-hero.webp`
- Version: `2.7.0`

## Visual System

- Background: graphite black `#070806`
- Surface: `#10120f`
- Primary text: off-white `#f3f4ee`
- Muted text: `#a4a79e`
- Accent: lime `#b8fb45`
- Borders: white at 10-14% opacity
- Typography: Space Grotesk
- Radius: 8px for cards, 16px for major media, pills only for controls and tags
- Motion: short opacity and translate transitions; reduced-motion fallback required

## Voice

Clear, direct, practical, and specific. Do not use invented clients, awards,
testimonials, results, revenue figures, or conversion claims. Label concept and
practice work explicitly.
