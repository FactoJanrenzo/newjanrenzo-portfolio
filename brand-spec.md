# Janrenzo Facto Brand Spec

## Identity

- Browser favicon: `public/favicon.svg`
- Header and footer avatar: `public/brand/janrenzo-avatar.webp` (round, thin lime ring)
- Primary portrait: `public/brand/janrenzo-hero.webp`
- Version: `2.7.0`

## Visual System

- Background: near-black `#0a0a0a`; raised surfaces `#111111`
- Primary text: `#ededed`; muted text `#a3a3a3`; captions `#8b8b8b`
- Accent: lime `#b8fb45`, with a 14% lime glow for backgrounds
- Borders: white at 8-16% opacity
- Typography: Geist for headings and body, Geist Mono for labels, numbers, and metadata (self-hosted)
- Radius: 20-24px for cards and media stages, 12px for fields and device frames, pills for buttons, chips, and filters
- Project media: screenshots shown inside browser or laptop frames on a dark stage; design work framed as artwork
- Motion: eased reveals, mouse-only pointer effects (background glow, cursor ring, card tilt), and Lottie process icons that play once; all motion off for reduced-motion settings and touch screens

## Voice

Clear, direct, practical, and specific. Do not use invented clients, awards,
testimonials, results, revenue figures, or conversion claims. Label concept and
practice work explicitly.
